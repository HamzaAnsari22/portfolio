01 — About

Engineering that turns business goals into reliable systems

I'm a backend-focused full stack engineer who designs and delivers scalable web platforms. My core is Node.js, NestJS, Express and TypeScript: REST API design, asynchronous service architecture and real-time data pipelines. I also build responsive front ends in React and Angular that plug cleanly into those services.

I work directly with clients. I gather requirements and turn business needs into technical specs the whole team can build against, and I run triage and release tracking so things ship on schedule.
